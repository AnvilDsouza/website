Strategic Playbook: The Logic Behind the Design

💡 First Principles

  • Single Truth: Identity extracted once, referenced everywhere.
  • Risk-Adjusted: Oversight scales with the risk score, not one-size-fits-all.
  • Fast-Track: Move from "Approved" to "Active" in the shortest safe time.

🤖 Where AI Wins

  • Noise Reduction: Filtering out 90% of false positive sanctions hits.
  • Consistency: Generating standardized suitability rationales.
  • Propensity: Identifying the best next-action for new users.

⚖️ Where Humans Lead

  • Ambiguity: Making the call on "Grey Zone" ID matches.
  • High Stakes: Signing off on large exposures or PEPS.
  • Trust: The first "Welcome" call for high-value clients.

📐 Design Rules

  • Minimise Free Text: Unstructured data breaks automation.
  • Confidence Bands: Explicitly define where the machine stops and human starts.
  • Explainability: Always store the "Why" (reason code) with the decision.
