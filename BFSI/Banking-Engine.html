The Hybrid Banking Model

A step-by-step breakdown of how AI Speed and Human Insight collaborate to run a modern bank.

AI Value: Scale, Speed, Data
Human Loop: Judgment, Trust, Exceptions
STEP 01

Establish Identity 🆔

Confirms who the client is. No service is safe without a verified identity.

🤖 AI: Validation & Matching
Instantly verifies documents via OCR and biometrics. Detects duplicates across millions of records.
👤 Human: Conflict Resolution
Decides on "grey zone" matches or data conflicts.
Rule: Escalate merge if 90% attributes match
STEP 02

Understand Risk ⚖️

Evaluates danger level. Risk determines limits, pricing, and monitoring.

🤖 AI: Real-Time Scoring
Calculates risk scores dynamically using transaction history and external data feeds.
Rule: Auto-flag if Risk Score > 70
👤 Human: Enhanced Due Diligence
Conducts deep-dive investigations on high-risk profiles and defines risk appetite.
STEP 03

Configure Products 📦

Matches client needs with products (loans, cards) to generate revenue.

🤖 AI: Recommendation Engine
Analyzes spending to suggest the "Next Best Product" or optimize pricing tiers.
Rule: Exclude premium cards if Income < X
👤 Human: Complex Structuring
Designs bespoke solutions for wealth clients and ensures ethical sales practices.
STEP 04

Take or Deploy Money 💰

Manages the balance sheet via deposits and loans.

🤖 AI: Credit Decisioning
Auto-approves low-value loans and forecasts liquidity needs instantly.
👤 Human: Large Exposure Approval
Approves high-value loans or exceptions to policy.
Rule: Manual sign-off if LTV > 80%
STEP 05

Execute Transactions ⚡

Processes payments and transfers. Accuracy determines client trust.

🤖 AI: Fraud Detection
Screens transactions in milliseconds for fraud and sanctions violations.
👤 Human: 2nd Factor Auth
Verifies high-value or suspicious transfers before release.
Rule: 2FA required for > ₹10L
STEP 06

Maintain Relationship 🤝

Handles queries and advice. Relationships drive Lifetime Value.

🤖 AI: Sentiment & Assist
Resolves Tier-1 queries via chat and drafts responses for advisors.
👤 Human: Empathy & Trust
Manages complex problems and emotional client situations.
Rule: Log stated intent for every call
STEP 07

Monitor Behavior 📡

Watches for unusual patterns, AML issues, or credit deterioration.

🤖 AI: Pattern Recognition
Monitors 24/7 to find "needle in a haystack" anomalies without fatigue.
Rule: Alert on 3 anomalies in 24h
👤 Human: Investigation
Contextualizes alerts to determine if they are fraud or just unusual behavior.
STEP 08

Records & Reporting 🗄️

Keeps accurate books and audit trails for regulatory scrutiny.

🤖 AI: Auto-Documentation
Generates audit trails and regulatory report drafts automatically.
👤 Human: Accountability
Provides final sign-off and ensures reason codes are interpretable.
Rule: Store human-readable reason codes
